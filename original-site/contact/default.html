Remuneration Policy for Board members and volunteers.

1. Board members

The Ma-Flo Foundation does not reward Board members financially, in kind or otherwise for their duties according to their functions in the Ma-Flo Foundation.

2. Volunteers

The Ma-Flo Foundation does not reward volunteers financially, in kind or otherwise for their duties according to their assignments or assistance in the Ma-Flo Foundation.

3. Costs incurred for the Ma-Flo Foundation

Board members and volunteers may claim reimbursement of costs incurred for the Ma-Flo Foundation, e.g. materials, postage, transport costs for goods or the like when such has been agreed upon beforehand with the Chairperson, Secretary or Treasurer of the Foundation.

This policy has been agreed upon by the Board on 10/11/2020 and is valid till repealed.
